# User Research — Australian Removalist Customers
**Date:** 2026-08-01 09:02 IST
**Topic:** Who we're designing for, what they fear, what makes them book
**Status:** Context for the Stellar redesign. The user base drives every decision.

---

## 1. The emotional reality

Moving house ranks alongside divorce and bereavement as life's most stressful events. In Australia the industry has a structural **trust gap**: anyone with an ABN and a truck can operate as a removalist with no mandatory licensing or insurance. Customers feel like they're gambling with their possessions.

Three psychological states:
1. **Pre-move dread** — fear of scams, hidden fees, ghosting.
2. **On-day anxiety** — crew not showing up, clock-milking, rough handling.
3. **Post-move resentment** — damage + claims being ignored.

**Design implication:** every element must *reduce anxiety*, not decorate. Calm > flashy. Concrete proof > abstract claims.

## 2. Decision criteria (in priority order)

1. **Pricing transparency** — no hidden fees. The #1 destroyer of trust.
2. **Reputation** — real reviews on Google / ProductReview, not cherry-picked quotes.
3. **Punctuality** — critical around lease end / settlement dates.
4. **Insurance & asset protection** — goods-in-transit vs public liability distinction.
5. **Careful handling** — blankets, straps, shrink-wrap, dollies, custom crates.
6. **Proactive communication** — confirmation emails, SMS tracking, call-backs.

## 3. What customers actually complain about (from review analysis)

*Note: the percentages below are directional estimates from a single pass over public review patterns — treat as guidance for content priorities, not audited statistics.*

- **Communication breakdowns** (~50% of 1-star reviews): crews late without a call; office unresponsive on moving day.
- **Surprise fee creep / bait-and-switch** (~46%): hourly quotes ballooning; extra charges demanded at the destination.
- **Damage + claims ghosting** (~40%): gouged heritage floorboards, smashed items, then silence.
- **Inconsistent subcontractors**: unbranded truck, untrained crew.

## 4. Objections & fears → design response

| Fear | Website response |
|---|---|
| "Hidden fees" | Itemized rate card: what's included vs what's extra; no asterisks |
| "They'll damage my stuff" | Insurance badges + 3-step claims workflow + photos of protected items |
| "They won't show up" | Punctuality SLA + SMS tracking promise + real crew photos |
| "Rough handling" | Equipment kit list (blankets, straps), training, real team photos |
| "Melbourne access problems" | Local guidance: parking permits, tram clearances, lift bookings |

## 5. CRO research (homepage structure for a premium mover)

**Optimal section order:**
1. **Hero** — value prop + risk-reversal subhead + one primary CTA + Google 4.9★ inline.
2. **Trust/authority strip** — real partner logos or verified badges (100% insured · police-checked · ABN).
3. **Quote calculator** (the core conversion engine) — embed early, not hidden below the fold.
4. **How it works** — 3-step visual (quote → pack & protect → delivery).
5. **Differentiators / Why Stellar** — white-glove contrast vs "man with a van".
6. **Reviews with photos/video** — real Melbourne homes.
7. **Service areas** — REAL map + suburb grid (local SEO).
8. **Final CTA** — fixed-price promise + guaranteed response time.

**CTA discipline (Hick's Law):** one primary intent per view. Header: "Get a Quote" + phone. Hero: one primary CTA. Mobile: sticky bottom bar with "Get Quote" + "Call".

**Form psychology:**
- Multi-step quote forms outperform single giant forms (up to 3x completion; figure sourced from form-vendor case studies — treat as directional, not guaranteed).
- Ask low-friction questions first (from → to → size → date).
- Collect phone + name on the FINAL step, framed as "where do we send your fixed quote?"
- "Free Quote / Calculate My Move" converts higher than "Book Now" for new visitors; "Book" is for post-quote lock-in; "Call" for urgency.

**Mobile (70%+ of AU local bookings):** thumb-zone CTAs, 44px+ targets, postcode autofill, autofill support, sticky bottom action bar.

**Trust signal placement:** stars near hero & CTAs; insurance below quote form & in Why-Stellar; ABN + address in footer; real fleet/crew photos mid-page.
